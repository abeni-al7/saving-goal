# ADR 0000: Decision Title

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Owners: Name or team

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, and forces that make a decision necessary.

## Decision

State the chosen approach precisely enough that a future contributor can tell whether an implementation follows it.

## Alternatives Considered

List credible alternatives and the material reason each was not selected.

## Consequences

Describe the positive outcomes, tradeoffs, risks, operational impact, and follow-up work.

## Validation

Explain how the decision will be evaluated and what evidence could cause it to be revisited.
